How we work

One job, done properly

Each venture solves a single specific problem for the people who actually have it. No product tries to be a platform before it has earned the right.

Shipped, not announced

A thing is listed here when you can use it. Relic Ledger went live before it was ever mentioned, and everything after it works the same way.

Independent by design

Ventures share a company, not a codebase. Each one can be built, run and judged on its own, which is what keeps any single one from holding the others back.
